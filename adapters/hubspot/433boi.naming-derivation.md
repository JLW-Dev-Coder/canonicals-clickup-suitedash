## The ruling that governs every name on this form

> NO FACT WHOSE SUBJECT IS THE FILING ENTITY MAY TAKE A SHARED irs433_ NAME. The categories still describe the printed relation; the SCOPE is decided by the subject, and on this form the subject is the filing entity for every bound key without exception.

- **This form's subject:** the business entity that is filing - a corporation, partnership, LLC classified as a corporation, or other multi-owner/multi-member LLC
- **433a:** the individual taxpayer
- **433f:** the individual taxpayer, including any sole proprietorship they run
- **433aoi:** the individual taxpayer; its Sections 4 to 6 describe that individual's SOLE PROPRIETORSHIP, which for tax purposes is the individual

Ruling 6: would one property serving both forms ever have to hold two different values for one taxpayer at one moment? For every key on this form the answer is yes, because the two forms are filed by two different persons and the printed eligibility rules make it impossible for them to describe the same business.

THIS FORM REUSES NOTHING. Every one of its properties is form-specific, and the reason is not that its questions are new - most of them are not - but that its subject is.

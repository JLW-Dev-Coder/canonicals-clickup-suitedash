2. THE PAIRING KEY — AND WHY THE OBVIOUS ONE IS WRONG

   Keyed on the FULL FIELD PATH with the page container removed, pages 1 and 3 do NOT
   correspond: 66 tails match, 17 are on page 1 only and 17 on page 3 only.

   The 17 unmatched on each side are the same 17 cells. The intermediate subform is spelled
   differently on the two pages:

     page 1   form1[0].Page1_Part1[0].AccountNumber[0].AccountNumber1[0]
     page 3   form1[0].Page3_Part2[0].AccountingNumber[0].AccountNumber1[0]

   AccountNumber[0] on page 1, AccountingNumber[0] on page 3 — a typo in the IRS form's own
   field naming, on the taxpayer copy. It is RECORDED, never normalised away: an engine that
   silently repaired it would be deciding that the two names mean the same thing, which is
   the judgement [R-08] reserves for the printed page. The LEAF names are identical on both
   sides (AccountNumber1 .. AccountNumber17), which is why the stem key below pairs them and
   the tail key does not.

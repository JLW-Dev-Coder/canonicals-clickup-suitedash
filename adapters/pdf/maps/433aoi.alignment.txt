WHICH BLOCKS ARE HERE, AND WHY
------------------------------
correlate-labels.mjs answers "what text is nearest this widget" and its answer is good for
most of this form: 425 of 425 widgets got a label, none unlabelled. The blocks below are the
ones where that answer, while individually correct, is NOT ENOUGH — because one caption is
shared across widgets that mean different things. The test applied was mechanical: group the
correlated labels by page and count how many widgets share one. Repetition ACROSS THE ROWS of
a repeatable table is normal and is not here (the map separates those by slot). Repetition
across DIFFERENT FACTS in one column is the failure this report exists for.

  page 5, Section 6   11 widgets share "Round to the nearest whole dollar. Do not enter a
                      negative number..."  — the business income and expense column, printed
                      lines (12) through (29) plus Box C.
  page 6, Section 7   11 widgets share "Round to the nearest whole dollar." — the household
                      income and expense column, printed lines (30)-(38) and (39)-(51) plus
                      Boxes D, E and F. This block also carries the form's worst leaf names.
  page 7, Section 8    7 widgets share "Yes [No]", and Section 8 prints NO line markers at
                      all — its cells are referenced only as Box G, Box H and "Offer Amount".
  page 8, Section 9    3 widgets share "Yes [No]" and the page prints no line markers either.

NOT HERE, AND WHY NOT
---------------------
  page 1, Section 1   captions sit one per cell directly above, sharing a left edge. The
                      dependent grid repeats "Name"/"Age"/"Relationship" four times, which is
                      four ROWS of one table, not four facts under one caption.
  pages 2, 3, 4       the asset tables. "Minus loan balance", "Current market value" and
                      "X .8 = $" repeat, and again only across rows. Column identity is
                      unambiguous by x, and spot-checked against the leaf names on page 3's
                      Property2 row, where amount_of_mortgage / date_of_final /
                      how_title_held sit under exactly the captions they are named for.

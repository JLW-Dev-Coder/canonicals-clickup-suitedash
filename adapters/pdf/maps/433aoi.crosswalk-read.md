# 433-A(OIC) — first-pass crosswalk read

**This is a READ, not a binding.** No map is authored, no field is bound, no property exists.
Everything below is measured from the printed page of `adapters/pdf/forms/f433aoi.pdf`
(Rev. 4-2026, Cat. No. 55896Q, SHA-256 `F7766AF7F1E5034C13AD2D89FEBAC43F9D84106F70E050B05DB0B21B4A7CBB74`)
against `adapters/pdf/forms/f433a.pdf` (Rev. 6-2026, Cat. No. 20312N), using
`align-block.mjs` and `line-markers.mjs`. Every field path is quoted whole from
`topmostSubform[0]`.

The question put was: **433-A(OIC) is expected to reuse most of the 433-A map with a +3
expense-line offset. Does that hold?**

---

## 1. The +3 expense offset: HOLDS, for thirteen consecutive lines, then STOPS

Printed captions, both forms, read off the drawn text:

| 433-A | printed caption | 433-A(OIC) | printed caption |
|-------|-----------------|------------|-----------------|
| 36 | Food, Clothing and Misc. | **(39)** | Food, clothing, and miscellaneous |
| 37 | Housing and Utilities | **(40)** | Housing and utilities |
| 38 | Vehicle Ownership Costs | **(41)** | Vehicle loan and/or lease payment(s) |
| 39 | Vehicle Operating Costs | **(42)** | Vehicle operating costs |
| 40 | Public Transportation | **(43)** | Public transportation costs |
| 41 | Health Insurance | **(44)** | Health insurance premiums |
| 42 | Out of Pocket Health Care Costs | **(45)** | Out-of-pocket health care costs |
| 43 | Court Ordered Payments | **(46)** | Court-ordered payments |
| 44 | Child/Dependent Care | **(47)** | Child/dependent care payments |
| 45 | Life Insurance | **(48)** | Life insurance premiums |
| 46 | Current year taxes (Income/FICA) | **(49)** | Current monthly taxes |
| 47 | Secured Debts (Attach list) | **(50)** | Secured debts/Other |
| 48 | Delinquent State or Local Taxes | **(51)** | Delinquent state and/or local tax payment(s) |
| 49 | Other Expenses (Attach list) | — | **NO LINE** |
| 50 | Total Living Expenses (add lines 36-49) | Box E | Total Household Expenses |

**Where it breaks.**

1. **433-A line 49, "Other Expenses (Attach list)", has no 433-A(OIC) line.** The OIC form
   folds it into (50) "Secured debts/Other", whose printed caption reads "any loan where you
   pledged an asset as collateral not previously listed, government guaranteed student loan,
   employer required retirement or dues" and which carries its own free-text cell
   `topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page6[0].tab_order[2].Interest_Dividends[0]`
   captioned "List debt(s)/expense(s)". So 433-A's 47 and 49 both land on OIC (50) and the
   offset stops being a function.

2. **Two of the thirteen are the same DOLLAR under a different QUESTION.**
   - 433-A 38 "Vehicle Ownership Costs" vs OIC (41) "Vehicle loan and/or lease payment(s)" —
     the same monthly figure by a narrower name.
   - 433-A 46 "Current year taxes (Income/FICA)" vs OIC (49) "Current monthly taxes
     (e.g., monthly cost of federal, state, and local tax, personal property tax, etc.)" —
     the OIC line is BROADER. A 433-A record's line 46 does not fill OIC (49) completely.

3. **433-A's expense column has a second column beside it that OIC does not have at all.**
   Every 433-A expense line prints an "Allowable Expenses" cell next to the actual
   (`topmostSubform[0].Page4[0].IRS35[0]` and its siblings, 26 of them declared
   `_never_autofill` in `433a.map.json`). 433-A(OIC) prints no IRS-allowed column: instead its
   printed instruction says "For expenses claimed in boxes (39) and (45) only, you should list
   the full amount of the allowable standard even if the actual amount you pay is less." The
   allowance is folded INTO the taxpayer's own figure on two lines and nowhere else. That is a
   semantic difference the offset conceals.

**Verdict on the offset: usable as an authoring aid for thirteen lines, not as a rule.**
Lines (39)-(51) map to 36-48 one for one; 49 has no home; and the meaning of (41), (49) and
the missing allowed column all need a decision, not an arithmetic shift.

## 2. The income side is NOT +3, and is not an offset at all

433-A prints income on lines **21-35** as one line per source. 433-A(OIC) prints income on
**(30)-(38)** and reshapes it: lines (30) and (31) are a **four-across grid per person** —
Wages / Social Security / Pension(s) / Other Income, once for the primary taxpayer and once
for "Spouse/Other" — each totalled into the (30)/(31) cell:

```
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page6[0].Primary[0].Wages[0].Primary_Taxpayer_Wages[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page6[0].Primary[0].Social[0].Primary_Taxpayer_Social_Security[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page6[0].Primary[0].Pensions[0].Primary_Taxpayer_Pensions[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page6[0].Primary[0].Other[0].Primary_Taxpayer_Other_Income[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page6[0].Primary[0].Total_Primary_Taxpayer_Income[0]        <- line (30)
```

433-A splits taxpayer and spouse into separate LINES (26/27 pension and social security
taxpayer, 29/30 spouse); the OIC form splits them into separate ROWS of one grid and asks for
a per-person total the 433-A form never computes. Any crosswalk here is per fact, not per line.

Also present on the OIC income half and absent from 433-A as its own line:
**(32) "Additional sources of income used to support the household, e.g., non-liable spouse,
or anyone else who may contribute"**, with a "List source(s)" free-text cell.

## 3. The structural break the offset cannot see: quick sale value

**433-A(OIC) discounts every asset to 80% of fair market value on the page.** Eight printed
`X .8 = $` columns exist and 433-A has no column of that kind anywhere:

```
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page3[0].Property1[0].current_market_value[0].times_8[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page3[0].Property2[0].current_market_value[0].times_8[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page3[0].Vehicle1[0].CurrentMarket[0].Times_8[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page3[0].Vehicle2[0].CurrentMarket[0].Times_8[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page4[0].Section3[0].OtherAsset1[0].CurrentMarketValue[0].Times_8[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page4[0].Section3[0].OtherAsset2[0].CurrentMarketValue[0].Times_8[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page5[0].Section5[0].Asset1[0].CurrentMarket[0].Times_8[0]
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page5[0].Section5[0].Asset2[0].CurrentMarket[0].Times_8[0]
```

A backbone real-property or vehicle row carries `current_fmv`; it does not carry the quick
sale value, and deriving it is a multiplication the taxpayer signs for. This is a NEW canonical
column, not a crosswalk of an existing one, and it belongs to `asset-row-shapes.json`'s
`real_property`, `vehicle`, `personal_property` and `business_property` classes alike.

The same asset blocks add **an allowance the OIC grants and 433-A does not**:
`topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page3[0].Enter_Allowance[0]` at printed line **6e** — the
vehicle equity allowance — and line **10**
(`topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page5[0].Section5[0].TotalValue[0].Line10[0]`), the business
asset deduction.

## 6. What Principal should decide before the map is authored

1. **`quick_sale_value` as a canonical column** on four asset classes — stored, or computed at
   fill time from `current_fmv`? It prints on a statement signed under penalty of perjury.
2. **The vehicle equity allowance (line 6e) and business asset deduction (line 10)** — IRS
   figures like the 433-A allowed column, or taxpayer input?
3. **433-A line 49 "Other Expenses"** — does it feed OIC (50) alongside line 47, and if so what
   composes the "List debt(s)/expense(s)" cell?
4. **The per-person income grid** on (30)/(31) — four new facts per person, or a re-shape of
   the taxpayer/spouse pension and social security facts the backbone already holds?
5. **Section 8** is arithmetic, not intake. Confirm it is filled by computation and declared in
   `433aoi.totals.json`, not provisioned.

## 7. Two traps this form sets, both measured

**The leaf names on page 6 lie, and they lie plausibly.** Three widgets are named
`Interest_Dividends` and only one of them is interest and dividends:

```
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page6[0].Interest_Dividends[0]                printed line (32)  Additional sources of income
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page6[0].tab_order[1].Interest_Dividends[0]   printed line (32)  "List source(s)" free text
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page6[0].Interest_Dividends[1]                printed line (33)  Interest, dividends, and royalties   <- the only true one
topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page6[0].tab_order[2].Interest_Dividends[0]   printed line (50)  "List debt(s)/expense(s)" free text
```

The fourth is on the EXPENSE half of the page, seventeen lines away, and a map author binding
by name would print interest income into a debt description on a filed collection statement.
`Name_Creditor[0..3]` on page 7 is reused across two unrelated rows the same way.

**Only 13 of 425 fields declare a `/MaxLen`.** 433-A declares one on 328 of 441 text fields;
this form declares almost none, so the ceiling discipline that `asset-row-shapes.json` records
per class does not transfer. The thirteen that do are exactly the fields carrying legacy
433-A-style leaf names:

```
/MaxLen  4  topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page1[0].Section1[0].dependentListing[0].Row1[0].Age_01[0]   (and Row2-4)
/MaxLen  8  topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page7[0].p1_t37_7[0]
/MaxLen  8  topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page7[0].p1_t38_7[0]
/MaxLen  8  topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page7[0].p1_t38_7[1]
/MaxLen  8  topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page7[0].p2_t50_8[0]
/MaxLen  8  topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page7[0].p2_t51_8[0]
/MaxLen 12  topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page7[0].p1-t42_10[0]
/MaxLen 12  topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page7[0].#subform[8].p1-t48_9[0]
/MaxLen 12  topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page8[0].past_10_years[0].#subform[0].p3_t2_11[0]
```

An absent `/MaxLen` is not permission to write anything: the printed box still has a width, and
a value that overflows it prints outside the rule on the filed page without the engine's hard
stop firing. Column ceilings for this form have to come from the widget RECT, which
`align-block.mjs` reports.

## 8. And the thing that did NOT carry over

On 433-A every internal leaf suffix lags the printed line by exactly one, form-wide — printed
36 is `p3_t58_35`, printed 37 is `p3_t60_36`, printed 51 is `p3_t85_50`.

**Measured on 433-A(OIC): the lag does not exist, because the names carry no line references.**
Of 80 printed line markers attached to a widget, **75** have leaf names with no line token at
all (`Food_Clothing_Misc`, `Total_Amount_1`); **2** carry one and it AGREES exactly
(`topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page3[0].line6b[0]` under printed 6b, and
`topmostSubform[0].F433-A-OIC_Page5[0].Section5[0].TotalValue[0].Line10[0]` under printed 10);
**3** carry a row ordinal, not a line reference (`Total_Amount_1/2/3` under printed 1a/1b/1);
and **0** lag.

So this form's names are semantic rather than positional. That removes the 433-A trap and
installs the one in §7 instead: a positional name that is wrong is obviously wrong once you
count, and a semantic name that is wrong reads as right.
